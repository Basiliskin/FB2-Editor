Privacy Policy for FB2 Editor

Last updated: 24 October 2026

FB2 Editor ("the app") is an editor for FictionBook (FB2) books on Android. This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short: your books stay on your device, the app has no accounts and no server of ours, and nothing leaves your device unless you use the optional AI feature described below.

Data the app handles

The app opens and saves the book files you choose. Your books, your notes and your settings are stored on your device. We do not receive, store or see any of it.

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and it does not collect your name, location, contacts or device identifiers. It has no user accounts.

AI-assisted improvement (optional)

FB2 Editor can send text from your book to an AI service so that it can suggest improvements. This feature is off until you add your own AI provider (Google Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic, or a compatible service at an address you enter) and your own API key in Settings.

When you ask for an improvement, the app sends the paragraphs you selected, the book's title and author, your style note and your instruction directly from your device to the provider you chose. Nothing is sent without your action, and the app asks for your confirmation the first time you use a provider. We do not operate a server for this feature and we do not receive, store or see your text or your key.

What the provider does with the text is governed by that provider's own terms and privacy policy, which you accept when you create your account and key with them.

Your API key is stored only on your device, in the Android secure storage. It is not saved in your book files, exports or backups made by the app. The connection to the provider is encrypted (https).

Source code

The app's source code is public, so anyone can check that it behaves as described here: github.com/Basiliskin/FB2-Editor.

Children

The app does not address anyone under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
